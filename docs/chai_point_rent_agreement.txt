Leave and licence agreement - shop premises (summary)

Licensor: Sunita Deshmukh. Licensee: Chai Point (proprietor Aasim Malik).
Premises: Shop no. 4, ground floor, Lane 7, Koregaon Park, Pune 411001, about 320 sq ft.

Term: 11 months, from 1 April 2026 to 28 February 2027, renewable by mutual consent.
Monthly licence fee: Rs 38,000, payable by the 5th of each month by bank transfer.
Late payment: Rs 500 per day after the 10th.
Security deposit: Rs 2,00,000, refundable within 30 days of handing back the premises.
Increase on renewal: 8% on the monthly fee.
Notice period: either side may end the agreement with 2 months' written notice.
Electricity and water are paid by the licensee as per the meter; maintenance of Rs 2,500 a month is included in the fee.
